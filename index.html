About Me

Hello, I am Zhenhuan Huang, a fourth-year student at the University of Toronto Mississauga, majoring in Communication, Culture, Information & Technology and Economics. My work explores how web development, visual communication, and economic thinking can come together to create useful and engaging experiences.

Selected Projects

My projects include a responsive portfolio website, the interactive web experience Heartbeat City, and The Price You Don’t See, a visual campaign about the hidden costs of fast fashion. These projects combine front-end development, interface design, and research.

Card 3

Interests & Practice

I am interested in responsive web design, interactive media, and visual storytelling. Through my coursework, I explore how clear interfaces and thoughtful design choices shape the way people understand information and interact with digital experiences.
